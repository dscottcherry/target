A clear path forward

Your foundation for AI starts with three everyday skills.

Each lesson uses plain language, guided practice, and time for questions. You’ll understand not only what to tap or click, but why.

01

Device literacy

Become more confident with a tablet, smartphone, or computer and the everyday controls you’ll use in class.

  • Touch, mouse, and keyboard basics
  • Settings, Wi-Fi, and accessibility
  • Comfort with everyday technology
02

Online safety & security

Learn simple habits that protect your accounts, personal information, and peace of mind while you explore online.

  • Stronger passwords
  • Scam and phishing awareness
  • Safer browsing decisions
03

Internet & app navigation

Practice searching, opening links, using apps, and recognizing the basic parts of an AI conversation.

  • Search and web navigation
  • Connected tools and apps
  • A clear pathway to AI

Who is this for?

Curious beginners are in the right place.

“What if I’m not very comfortable with technology yet?”

That is exactly why Step 1 exists. Bring your questions, your device if you have one, and a willingness to learn. The course is designed for adults at the beginning of their technology and AI journey.

  • Adults returning to technology
  • Older learners who prefer a comfortable pace
  • Anyone who wants safer, more confident online habits
